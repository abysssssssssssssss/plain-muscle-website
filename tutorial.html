Tutorial

Get started with Plain Muscle in five steps.

Step 1

Log your first workout

The same steps as the tutorial in the app.

  1. Tap Calendar, then tap today's date
  2. Name your workout and tap Done
  3. Tap Use saved workout to pick a template, or Add Exercise to build one
  4. Enter reps and weight for each set
  5. Tap Save. The day now shows as logged
Step 2

Set your weekly goal

Pick how many days a week you want to train.

  1. Open Home
  2. Find This week's workout progress
  3. Tap the pencil and set your goal
  4. Every logged day fills the bar
Step 3

Turn on Focus mode

Strip the app down to your goal and your calendar.

  1. Open Profile
  2. Go to Customization
  3. Turn on Focus mode
Step 4

Lock In

Ready to commit? Read the rules first: you can't cancel once you sign up.

  1. Tap Locked In Mode on Home
  2. Choose your duration, from 1 week to 1 year
  3. Check the start and end dates on the calendar
  4. Tap Sign up for Lock In while signup is open
  5. Log workouts on at least 3 days every week, and never go 6 days without one

Step 5

Climb the ranks

Every workout counts. Finished Lock In time earns the Locked In ranks.

  1. Log workouts to move from Iron toward Muscle
  2. Sign each new badge in your Rank Book
  3. See your records and charts in Peaks

In the app, open Profile and tap Tutorial to replay it.
