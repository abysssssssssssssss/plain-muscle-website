Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-05 · Version 2026-11-05

1. Plain Muscle and contact

Plain Muscle helps you plan workouts and record personal fitness activity. This policy explains the information used by the app and its account services. For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion help, contact the Plain Muscle team at official.plain.muscle@gmail.com. You can read this policy without creating an account.

2. Information you provide

Account information includes your email address, account identifier, profile name and photo, language, appearance, goals, and reminder preferences. Fitness records can include workout plans and logs, exercises, sets, weights, notes, body measurements, step and calorie entries, and photos or videos you choose to add. These records may reveal information about your health or physical condition. Enter only information you want to use with the service and avoid including other people’s information without permission.

At signup, your date of birth and country or region of residence are used to check eligibility on the server. Your full birth date is not saved with your account. We save your age band and country or region, eligibility status, and records of your Terms agreement and optional marketing choice. Those records include the wording and version shown, language, source of the choice, and server timestamp. If you email support, the message, contact information, and attachments you send are part of that correspondence.

3. Why information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, recover passwords, store and sync the records you choose, display progress and activity summaries, manage reminders, check signup eligibility, record your choices, and respond to support and privacy requests. Calculations depend on the information you enter.

Your agreement to the Terms is separate from permission for marketing. Optional product news and offers by email require your separate opt-in. Your workout records and body measurements are not used to decide your marketing choice. Necessary security, password recovery, and account service emails are separate from optional marketing.

Where these bases apply in your jurisdiction, we rely on performance of the account service contract for processing necessary to provide the service you request; legitimate interests in protecting accounts and preventing service abuse for security processing, subject to your rights; and your consent for optional email marketing. Health information may require an additional condition under applicable law. Agreement to the Terms or reading this policy does not itself establish that condition or provide any separately required explicit health-data consent.

4. Firebase, service providers, and international processing

Account sign-in and password recovery use Google Firebase Authentication. Account records, eligibility and consent records, and synced fitness data are stored in Cloud Firestore. Selected profile photos and workout media are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage when syncing. Cloud Functions process signup, eligibility, and marketing preference requests. These services process information to provide authentication, storage, and account functions; network requests also expose technical information such as your IP address to the service receiving them. Email support uses the support mailbox provider.

To limit signup abuse, we store an attempt count keyed by a daily rotated SHA-256 digest of the server-observed IP address and UTC date. The counter does not store the raw IP address and is separate from your account, but it is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. Each counter has an expiry timestamp set 24 hours ahead. Automatic cleanup is asynchronous when the provider’s time-to-live (TTL) feature is enabled; an expiry timestamp alone does not delete the record or guarantee removal exactly at 24 hours.

Provider processing may take place outside your country or region, depending on the service configuration and provider operations. Contact official.plain.muscle@gmail.com for available information about providers and international processing. This policy does not represent that a particular storage country or transfer arrangement has been verified for every user’s location. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to respond to a valid legal request.

5. Device storage and permissions

The app keeps account caches and pending changes on your device using AsyncStorage and local files so you can work while offline. A change may remain only on that device until sync succeeds. Check Cloud sync in Account for pending changes. Clearing app data or uninstalling before sync finishes can lose those changes.

Photo or camera access is requested when you choose to add media. Notification permission is used for local workout reminders scheduled on your device. You can turn reminders off in Preferences and change permissions in device settings. Declining optional permissions may limit the related feature. Changing notification permission does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

6. Optional email and withdrawing consent

The marketing choice starts unchecked. You can create and use an account without opting in, and withdrawing does not remove access to the app. Change Email preferences in Account and save, use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email, or ask for help at official.plain.muscle@gmail.com. A successful save or unsubscribe confirmation records the change on the server. If it fails or you are offline, retry; a change on screen alone does not confirm that your choice has been saved. Withdrawal applies to future optional marketing and does not affect earlier processing based on your choice. Essential account emails are separate.

7. Retention and account deletion

Your account and fitness records remain available while retained in your account, until you remove the records or delete the account. Retention decisions for support correspondence and technical service records depend on the need to answer the request, maintain service security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations; no single fixed period applies to all record types. Contact us to ask about a particular record.

Delete account in Account removes your Firebase sign-in account, associated cloud records, eligibility and consent records, and uploaded media, as well as account caches and reminders on the device where you complete deletion. Deletion needs a connection and may need a retry if interrupted; some data may already be removed before a failure is reported. Original photos and videos in your photo library remain. Copies cached on other devices are not remotely erased: remove the app data on those devices if needed. Emails you sent to support are held separately from the app account; include them in your deletion request if you want them considered.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit profile details and fitness records in the app and request account deletion from Account. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also be able to complain to your local data protection authority.

Email official.plain.muscle@gmail.com from your account email where possible, describe the request, and identify the records concerned. We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership before disclosing or changing personal information. Do not send your password. Requests are considered under applicable law, including any permitted exceptions and deadlines.

9. Age eligibility

New accounts currently require an age of at least 18. A lower regional age can be enabled only after the applicable signup requirements have been reviewed, and the platform minimum will never be below 13. Marketing to anyone under 18 is disabled unless separately reviewed for the relevant region. There is currently no reviewed exception. If you believe an ineligible child has provided information, contact official.plain.muscle@gmail.com so the account and deletion request can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

The effective date and version appear above. We will publish updated text here and notify users in the app or through account contact details of material changes before they take effect where required. A new purpose requiring consent will require a separate choice; a policy update alone does not opt you into marketing.

11. Store purchases and billing records

The billing service creates account-binding identifiers and, when purchases are available, uses Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing. The store collects your payment details; Plain Muscle does not collect card numbers or security codes. Our Firebase backend receives store product and transaction identifiers, Google purchase tokens where applicable, purchase status, renewal and expiry dates, and store notifications. We use these records to verify purchases, restore access, prevent a purchase being claimed by another app account, and handle subscription changes or refunds. Purchase verification communicates with Apple or Google.

Deleting your app account removes its billing profile, account-binding mappings, and stored purchase proof. Minimal hashed account and transaction ownership markers are retained to prevent deleted or previously claimed purchases from being reused. These markers are pseudonymous and are not promised to be anonymous. Their retention and any legally required billing records are subject to applicable law and the review described in section 7. App account deletion does not delete the store’s records or cancel a store subscription.

12. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn on Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) in Profile, the app reads your daily step count and active calories burned for today and the previous 7 days. It requests read access only and never writes to, changes, or deletes your health data. The daily totals are saved as the steps taken and calories burned on your calendar days and sync with your account like other fitness records. They are not used for advertising or marketing, are not sold, and are not shared with third parties except the service providers described in section 4 that store your account data.

You can turn syncing off in Profile at any time. Values already saved stay on your calendar days until you edit or remove them or delete your account. To withdraw access, use the Health app on iPhone or Health Connect on Android.

13. Signing in with Google or Apple

If you choose Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, that company confirms your identity and gives our Firebase Authentication service a sign-in token. The token includes an account identifier and your email address. Apple may give a private relay address instead if you choose Hide My Email. We use this information only to create and secure your Plain Muscle account. Google and Apple handle your use of their sign-in under their own privacy policies. You can stop using that sign-in method in your Google or Apple account settings; you may then need another way to sign in to Plain Muscle.

14. Crash reports and diagnostics

The app uses Sentry to find and fix crashes, errors, and slow screens. When an error happens, and for a sample of other sessions, the app sends Sentry diagnostic information: device model, operating system, app version, the screens you opened, app logs, performance timings, and the IP address of the connection. For about 1 in 10 sessions, and for sessions where an error occurs, the app also sends a replay of the screens shown. Text, images, and what you type are masked in replays by default.

This information is used only to keep the app working. It is not used for advertising or marketing and is not sold. Sentry may process it outside your country or region, as described in section 4. Retention follows the criteria in section 7; contact official.plain.muscle@gmail.com to ask about a particular report.

15. Lock In records

If you sign up for Lock In, our Firebase backend stores your chosen duration, signup, start and end times, session status, the days credited with a workout, missed-day counts, and the reason a session ended. A scheduled server task checks these records every day at 7 AM UTC+7 to apply the Lock In rules. Lock In records are part of your account data: they are kept while your account exists and are removed when you delete your account, as described in section 7.

16. This website

This policy also covers plainmuscle.com. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit, as with any website request. The website has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking cookies. It saves only your light or dark display choice in your browser’s storage, which you can clear in your browser settings. Email links on the website open your own email app; messages you send are handled as support correspondence.
