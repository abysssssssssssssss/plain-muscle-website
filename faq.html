FAQs

Quick answers about Plain Muscle.

Access and data

Is Plain Muscle free?

Plain Muscle is free for 3 days after you create your account. When the trial ends, choose a paid plan in the app to continue using it.

Is my data private?

Your workout records are stored under your account. Plain Muscle uses Firebase to sign you in and sync your data. You can delete your account and synced records from Profile → Account → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what we store, who processes it, and how deletion works.

What platforms does Plain Muscle support?

Plain Muscle supports iPhone and Android phones.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can log workouts offline, and your changes sync when you reconnect. For Locked In credit, check off at least one set and save that day. The credit must reach the server before 7 AM UTC+7.

Does my data sync to the cloud?

Yes. Sign in to the same Plain Muscle account on a new phone to access your synced workouts. Before switching phones or reinstalling, check Profile → Account → Cloud sync and let any pending changes finish syncing.

Do I need Apple Health or Health Connect?

Connecting either service is optional. If you turn it on in Profile, Plain Muscle reads your daily steps and active calories for today and the previous 7 days and adds those totals to your calendar. The app only reads from those services.

Using Plain Muscle

Where is Focus Mode?

Open Profile → Preferences → Focus mode and turn it on.

Where do I find Rank Themes?

Open Profile → Preferences → Rank theme to browse themes, apply one you’ve unlocked, or turn on the Day Randomizer.

How do I log a workout?

Tap a date in Calendar, then choose a saved workout or add your own exercises. Enter your reps and weight, check off the sets you complete, and tap Save. You can also tap Add a workout for today in Plans or Focus Mode. See the workout tutorial for a walkthrough.

Lock In and ranks

Can I cancel a Lock In?

Once you sign up, the commitment is final. You can review the duration and dates before you sign up.

Do workouts count toward my Classic rank while I’m Locked In?

Yes. Workouts you save on the day you train still count toward your Classic rank during a Lock In.

What happens if I fail a Lock In?

Your session ends and its time stops counting toward Promised ranks. You keep your workout logs and Classic rank progress.

Will there be more or exclusive Rank Themes?

Classic and Promised ranks already have separate theme collections. Use Classical Orbs for Classic themes and Promised Orbs for Promised themes. Check Updates for announcements about new themes.
