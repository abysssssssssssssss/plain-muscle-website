FAQs

Quick answers about Plain Muscle.

General

Is it free?

Pricing will be announced at launch in December 2026.

Which platforms is it on?

iPhone and Android phones.

Which languages does it support?

English, Chinese and Indonesian.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your phone and sync to your account when you're back online. Don't clear the app's data or uninstall it before syncing finishes, or unsynced changes can be lost.

Lock In

Can I cancel a Lock In?

No. Once you sign up for a Lock In session, you can't cancel. Signup opens 3 days before the session starts and closes when it begins, so you can preview your plan first.

What happens if I miss a day?

Missing a day is fine. The session only ends if you log workouts on fewer than 3 days in a full 7-day block, or go 6 days in a row without a workout. If it ends, that session's Lock In time and rank progress are voided, but your workouts and Classic progress stay.

What counts as a workout day?

Check off at least one set and save that day. The workout has to reach our server before 7 AM UTC+7, and each day counts once.

When do sessions start and end?

At 7 AM UTC+7. The end date shown is the 7 AM finish.

Ranks

How do I earn Classic ranks?

Log workouts. Iron comes with your first, and Muscle, the top Classic rank, at 300.

How do I earn Locked In ranks?

With your total Lock In time: Zenus at 1 month, Eldron at 3 months, Primalus at 6 months, Ascendor at 1 year and Honorado at 2 years.

Your data

Can it sync with Apple Health or Health Connect?

Yes. Turn it on in Profile, and the app reads your daily steps and active calories for today and the past 7 days. It only reads; it never changes your health data.
