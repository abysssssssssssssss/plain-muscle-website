About Us

Gym apps got bloated. Plain Muscle stays plain.

Most fitness apps want you scrolling feeds, chasing streak notifications, and paying for features you'll never use. We wanted the opposite: a tracker that gets out of the way while you train, and quietly rewards you for coming back.

So Plain Muscle does a few things well. Plan your split. Log your sets. Watch your rank climb. And when you're ready to prove it, lock in.

The mascot is asleep for now. It wakes up in December 2026.
