Features

Everything Plain Muscle does, and nothing it doesn't need to.

Focus UI

Turn on Focus mode and the app shrinks to what matters: this week's goal and the days you trained.

  • Weekly workout goal front and center
  • Month calendar of every day you trained
  • Lock In one tap away
  • No feeds, no ads, no clutter
Only on Plain Muscle

Locked In Mode

Pick a commitment and stick to it. No other gym app holds you to your word like this.

  • Commit for 1 week, 2 weeks, 1, 3, 4 or 6 months, or a full year
  • Log workouts on at least 3 days in every 7-day block
  • Never go 6 days in a row without a workout
  • No cancelling once you sign up
  • Finish to earn exclusive Locked In ranks

Are you sure you want to lock in?

Logging & split planning

Build your weekly split once, then just show up and check off sets.

  • Today's exercises ready from your split
  • Check off sets as you go
  • Weight and reps for every set
  • Save once and the day is logged

Calendar

Every workout you save lands on your calendar, so you can see your consistency at a glance.

  • Month view of every session
  • Tap a day to see what you did
  • Lock In days marked on the calendar

Rank & badges

Every workout counts toward your next rank. Every badge you earn goes in your Rank Book.

  • 8 Classic ranks, from Iron to Muscle
  • 5 Locked In ranks, from Zenus to Honorado
  • Sign each new badge in your Rank Book
  • Rank-up animation every time you climb
