Features

Inside Plain Muscle

Find your way around the app, from the single-screen Focus Mode to the five tabs in Regular mode.

Main features↓ Regular tabs↓

Main

Choose what you see and how it looks.

Focus Mode

One screen for your monthly calendar, weekly goal, and Workout Gallery. Start logging today’s workout with one tap.

Rank Theme

Unlock themes with rank orbs. Each has its own art, fonts, and icons in Focus and Regular mode. Use the Day Randomizer to rotate unlocked themes.

  • Cyberpunk
  • Bauhaus
  • Celestial

Customization

Pick light or dark, choose the Default or Refined layout, and decide which details appear in the workout editor.

  • Navigation labels
  • Glass navigation
  • Default workout details

Regular

Explore the five tabs in the app.

Home Calendar Plans Progress Profile

Home

See what’s on for the week and how much you’ve trained.

Locked In Mode
Start a commitment or check your active session and the time you’ve locked in.
Your Week
See what’s scheduled in your weekly workout plan.
This Week’s Workout Progress
Check your logged workouts against your weekly goal.
Workout Gallery
Browse the photos you’ve saved with your workouts.

Calendar

Browse your training by month and open any day to see what you logged.

Log a Workout
Tap a date to add exercises and save a workout right from Calendar.
Red dot
Marks today in the default calendar before you log a workout.
★ Silver star
Shows that today’s workout counted toward your Classic rank.

Plans

Pick a template, build your own routine, or log a session on the spot.

Add a Workout Today
Use a saved workout or start from scratch.
Templates
Browse ready-made plans and add them to My Workouts.
My Workouts
Build and save your own workouts and training splits.
Workout Details
Log exercises, sets, weight, and reps. Add notes, biometrics, photos, and session details.

Progress

See how your lifts have changed and revisit the sessions that got you there.

Progress
Pick an exercise to see its graph and recent training days.
Records
Check workout stats, recent personal bests, and your workout history.
Achievements
See your Classic and Promised ranks, sign new badges in the Rank Book, and revisit your achievement history.

Profile

Manage your settings and account, or get help with the app.

Preferences
Set your notifications, appearance, and language. Connect Apple Health or Health Connect to see steps and calories.
Account
Edit your profile, manage email preferences and billing, check cloud sync, or reset your password.
Help & Information
Contact support, read the FAQs, replay the tutorial, and find app and legal information.
